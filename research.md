      <h3>Health Communication</h3>

      <p>
        My primary research interest lies in health communication,
        with a focus on how media environments and communication practices
        influence people's access to health information, social support,
        and psychological well-being.
      </p>

      <div class="research-keywords">
        <span>Health Information</span>
        <span>Social Support</span>
        <span>Media & Health</span>
      </div>
    </div>


    <div class="research-card featured">
      <div class="research-icon">♿</div>

      <h3>Accessible Communication</h3>

      <p>
        I examine communication accessibility and the ways in which
        media and communication environments shape the experiences,
        participation, and well-being of marginalized and underserved groups.
      </p>

      <div class="research-keywords">
        <span>Accessibility</span>
        <span>Marginalized Groups</span>
        <span>Inclusive Communication</span>
      </div>
    </div>


    <div class="research-card featured">
      <div class="research-icon">🧠</div>

      <h3>Media & Mental Health</h3>

      <p>
        I am particularly interested in the relationship between media,
        communication, and the mental health of marginalized groups,
        including how digital environments may provide opportunities
        for support while also producing new forms of vulnerability.
      </p>

      <div class="research-keywords">
        <span>Mental Health</span>
        <span>Digital Media</span>
        <span>Psychological Well-being</span>
